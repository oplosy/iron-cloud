# Failure test: cmp1

Started 2026-10-20 15:30. Power cut with `echo o > /proc/sysrq-trigger` (no sync, no clean shutdown); power restored with Hyper-V `Start-VM`.

## Findings

- Data written inside build before the failure is intact after evacuation (md5 25fa474f217756e69b971e074a965356).
- RADOS objects written before and during the failure read back with matching md5 after recovery.
- build was down from the power cut until it answered ping again on cmp2 (see timeline); Nova does not evacuate on its own.

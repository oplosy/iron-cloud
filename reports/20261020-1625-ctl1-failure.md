# Failure test: ctl1

Started 2026-10-20 16:19. Power cut with `echo o > /proc/sysrq-trigger` (no sync, no clean shutdown); power restored with Hyper-V `Start-VM`.

## Findings

- East-west (build -> web, same network): ping seq: 363 samples, longest gap 21; no samples in the last 21 s before the logs were read (had not resumed by then) (a gap of 1 means no loss).
- Routed traffic (build -> 1.1.1.1 via the router on ctl1): ping seq: 21 samples, longest gap 343 seconds; no samples in the last 21 s before the logs were read (had not resumed by then).
- Disk writes inside build (Ceph-backed volume): io writes: 348 samples, longest gap 29 seconds.

## Operator notes

- **The "last 21 s" tails above are a measurement bug, not an outage:** the script took ping's expected last seq from wall-clock time, but ping's interval drifts past 1 s in this VM (363 pings in 384 s). The logs show replies up to seq 363 on both probes. Corrected reading: east-west lost 0 of 363 pings; routed traffic was down for one 343 s gap and had resumed before the logs were read. The script now uses ping's own transmitted count.
- **Disk stall:** 29 s, Ceph's heartbeat grace before it marked ctl1's OSD down (27 s and 30 s in the earlier runs).
- **Power-on:** the UAC prompt took about 70 s this time (ctl1 back on the network at t+196). Measured from then: Keystone +145 s, all Nova services +171 s, Neutron agents +179 s.

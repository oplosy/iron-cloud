# Failure test: ctl1

Started 2026-10-20 15:42. Power cut with `echo o > /proc/sysrq-trigger` (no sync, no clean shutdown); power restored with Hyper-V `Start-VM`.

## Findings

- East-west (build -> web, same network): ping seq: 284 samples, longest gap 1 (a gap of 1 means no loss).
- Routed traffic (build -> 1.1.1.1 via the router on ctl1): ping seq: 7 samples, longest gap 1 seconds.
- Disk writes inside build (Ceph-backed volume): io writes: 273 samples, longest gap 30 seconds.

## Operator notes

- **Routed traffic line is misleading:** all 7 replies came before the power cut and none after, so the outage is the tail of the log, which the gap measure does not count. Routed traffic was down from t+0 until at least t+303, when the department's floating IP answered again through the rebuilt router; a ping to 1.1.1.1 from `build` right after the test had 0% loss.
- **Disk stall:** 30 s, the heartbeat grace before Ceph marked ctl1's OSD down (27 s on 2026-10-19).
- **After recovery:** the isolation test passed all checks; the stale security-group members seen on 2026-10-19 did not recur.
- **Power-on:** the UAC prompt was approved at once this time, so recovery here (Keystone +144 s, Nova +161 s, Neutron +170 s after ctl1 was back on the network) is the clean number.
